Join with phone

Scan your drawing and use your phone as a controller.
  1. Draw a character on white paper with a dark pen (colours welcome!). Close the outline.
  2. Open the big screen, then scan its QR code with each phone.
  3. Snap your drawing, describe its personality, and jump into Kuching.
  4. Collect kolo mee, find hidden cats, visit landmarks, dance on the Waterfront and high-five friends!
